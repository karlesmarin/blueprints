Part I collects five theorems and two propositions in the main text, eight calibration and bound theorems in Appendix K, six definitions and one empirical conjecture. The Isabelle proofs cover eight groups of the appendix; Lean verifies parts of four main-text results. None of these proofs establishes how a model is trained.
