The Pentagon, Machine-Checked: Abel’s five-term relation for the dilogarithm in Lean 4

🏷️ Special functions · the dilogarithm

Carles Marín

At a glance

What the paper contributes

  • A sorry-free Lean 4 proof of Abel's five-term relation for the Rogers $L$-function, $L(x)+L(y)=L(xy)+L\big(\tfrac{x(1-y)}{1-xy}\big)+L\big(\tfrac{y(1-x)}{1-xy}\big)$ for $x,y\in(0,1)$ (Theorem 1).
  • The route is the classical one: the five-term difference has zero derivative in $x$ (nine logarithms collapse into a basis of five), so it is constant, and its value is read off as $x\to0^+$, where $\log t\cdot\log(1-t)\to0$ by a squeeze.
  • Three reusable blocks on the dilogarithm of the companion paper: the derivative of $L$ on $(0,1)$, $L(t)\to0$ as $t\to0^+$, and continuity of $L$ on $(0,1)$.
  • The mathematics is classical (Spence, Abel, Rogers); the paper claims the formalization, after a documented search of proof-assistant libraries.

For all \(x,y\in (0,1)\),

\[ \operatorname{L}(x)+\operatorname{L}(y)=\operatorname{L}(xy)+\operatorname{L}\! \Big(\tfrac {x(1-y)}{1-xy}\Big) +\operatorname{L}\! \Big(\tfrac {y(1-x)}{1-xy}\Big). \]
