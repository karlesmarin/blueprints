🏷️ The labels

🟢 formal: the whole statement is machine-checked
🟡 formal in part: the rest is to be checked by hand
🔵 computation: written proof; its computations are checked by a program
🔴 by hand: written proof only
⚪ cited / no proof here: a result of others, or a question
