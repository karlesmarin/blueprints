Remark 7

One triple of quaternionic structures does not suffice: for \(z_{ij}=\sum _a\eta ^a_{ij}v_a\) with the ’t Hooft symbols \(\eta ^a\), the second variation is \(\sum _av_a^\top (\sum _jH_{jj})v_a\), because \((\eta ^a)^\top \eta ^b\) has symmetric part \(\delta _{ab}I\) and \(H_{jk}=H_{kj}\); it only says that \(\sum _jH_{jj}\succeq 0\). The argument above uses the whole of \(\Lambda ^2B\).

Lemma 4a regular point

Identify both factors with \({\mathbb {R}}^4\). Let \(S\subset V\) be the span of the four forms \(x_iy_i\) and the six forms \(x_iy_j+x_jy_i\) for \(i{\lt}j\). If \(H\) is a Hankel multiplier and \(S\subseteq \ker H\), then \(H=0\). Consequently \(d\Phi \) is surjective at the tuple consisting of these ten forms and zero.
