The exact value BSR(4,4) = 11: every biquadratic sum of squares in x1..4, y1..4 is a sum of eleven squares of bilinear forms. Qi and Cui gave a form that needs eleven; the previous upper bound was twelve. The proof classifies the Hankel multipliers of rank at most five whose image contains no product (Lemmas 2 and 3) and rules out the two shapes left with second variations built from the alternating forms (Propositions 6 and 8).
