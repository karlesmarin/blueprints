Lean docstring

d'Ocagne's identity for the Vieta–Fibonacci polynomials. For Mathlib's Polynomial.Chebyshev.S (the rescaled Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind, indexed by ℤ) over a commutative ring R, and all p q : ℤ, S p · S (q − 1) − S q · S (p − 1) = − S (p − q − 1). The case q = p + 1 is the Cassini-type identity Polynomial.Chebyshev.S_sq_add_S_sq; at X = 1 it is d'Ocagne's identity for Fibonacci numbers.

  • Archive: Orbit.S_docagne (Formalizations.RingTheory.Polynomial.Chebyshev.Docagne); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: d'Ocagne's identity for Fibonacci numbers, named after M. d'Ocagne; stated, e.g., in E. W. Weisstein, d'Ocagne's Identity, MathWorld.
  • Example: at p = 2, q = 1: S 2 · S 0 − S 1 · S 1 = −S 0, that is X² − 1 − X² = −1.

Upstream

Mathlib has the Chebyshev polynomials S and the Cassini-type identity S_sq_add_S_sq but not the two-index identity
