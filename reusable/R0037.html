  5. Fetch and check

    Shell

    lake update fetches this package and Mathlib, and Mathlib's update hook downloads the compiled Mathlib (lake exe cache get). lake lean Example.lean builds the imports of the file (steps 3 and 4) and checks it. If the file belongs to a lean_lib of your project, lake build <lib> does the same. Avoid a bare lake build in a project from the standard template: its default target is the executable, whose link compiles every imported Mathlib module to native code. Same versions required: Lean v4.35.0, Mathlib f0b4c81.

Alternative: import Formalizations.Reusable imports the whole layer (all areas).

For P_H(x, y) = (x₀² + x₁²)(y·y) + 2x₀x₁(y·Hy) over a commutative ring and DᵀD = 1, every sum-of-squares representation of P_H by bilinear forms x·(B_i y) transports to one of \(P_{DᵀHD}\) by the forms B_i D (same number of squares); signed-graph switching is D = diag(s), s_i² = 1.

Lean docstring

Sums of squares of bilinear forms under orthogonal congruence. For the biquadratic form P_H(x, y) = (x₀² + x₁²) (y ⬝ᵥ y) + 2 x₀ x₁ (y ⬝ᵥ H y) over a commutative ring and Dᵀ D = 1, every representation P_H = ∑ i (x ⬝ᵥ B i y)² gives P_{Dᵀ H D} = ∑ i (x ⬝ᵥ (B i D) y)² with the same number of squares; so the sum-of-squares length of P_H is invariant under H ↦ Dᵀ H D (signed-graph switching is D = diagonal s, s i² = 1: BiquadraticSOS.P_switching).

  • Archive: BiquadraticSOS.sos_transport (Formalizations.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.BiquadraticOrthogonal; also BiquadraticSOS.P_mulVec_orthogonal, BiquadraticSOS.P_switching); a piece of the design table (lengths of P_H, signed graphs).
  • Classical reference: change of variables y ↦ D y (textbook); the form P_H is that of Blekherman–Plaumann–Sinn–Vinzant and Hanselka–Sinn.
  • Example: with D = diagonal ![1, -1] the two-square representation of P_H transports to one of P_{D H D}.
