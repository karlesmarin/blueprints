  4. Paste the example

    Lean 4
    open Formalizations.Reusable Polynomial

/-- Budan–Fourier: if the derivative tower loses exactly one sign variation from `a` to `b`, then
`p` has a root in `(a, b]` (the parity forbids zero roots). -/
example (p : Polynomial ℝ) (hp : p ≠ 0) {a b : ℝ} (hab : a < b) (ha : p.eval a ≠ 0)
    (hb : p.eval b ≠ 0)
    (h1 : ((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval a)).signVariations -
        ((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval b)).signVariations = 1) :
    ∃ x ∈ Set.Ioc a b, p.IsRoot x := by
  obtain ⟨-, hev⟩ := budan_fourier_card_roots_le p hp hab ha hb
  rw [h1] at hev
  have hne : (p.roots.filter (fun x => a < x ∧ x ≤ b)).card ≠ 0 := by
    intro h0
    rw [h0] at hev
    simp at hev
  obtain ⟨x, hx⟩ := Multiset.card_pos_iff_exists_mem.1 (Nat.pos_of_ne_zero hne)
  rw [Multiset.mem_filter] at hx
  exact ⟨x, ⟨hx.2.1, hx.2.2⟩, (mem_roots hp).1 hx.1⟩

The Budan–Fourier theorem: for nonzero p ∈ ℝ[X] and a < b with p(a), p(b) ≠ 0, the number of roots of p in (a, b] counted with multiplicity is at most V(a) − V(b), with even difference, where V(x) is the number of sign variations of p, p′, …, p^(deg p) at x.

theorem budan_fourier_card_roots_le (p : Polynomial ℝ) (hp : p ≠ 0) {a b : ℝ} (hab : a < b)
    (ha : p.eval a ≠ 0) (hb : p.eval b ≠ 0) :
    (p.roots.filter (fun x => a < x ∧ x ≤ b)).card ≤
        ((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval a)).signVariations -
          ((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval b)).signVariations ∧
      Even (((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map
          (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval a)).signVariations -
        ((List.range (p.natDegree + 1)).map (fun k => ((derivative^[k]) p).eval b)).signVariations -

Lean docstring

The Budan–Fourier theorem. For a nonzero real polynomial p and a < b with p a ≠ 0 and p b ≠ 0, let V x be the number of sign variations (Mathlib's List.signVariations, zeros skipped) of the derivative tower p, p', p'', …, p^{(deg p)} evaluated at x. Then the number of real roots of p in (a, b], counted with multiplicity, is at most V a − V b, and the difference is even. Descartes' rule of signs is the case (0, ∞).

  • Archive: BudanFourier.budan_fourier (Formalizations.Analysis.Polynomial.RealRoots.BudanFourier), restated with List.signVariations through the archive bridges BudanFourier.fourierVar_eq_signVarAt and Sturm.signVarAt_eq_signVariations.
  • Classical reference: F. D. Budan (1807), J. Fourier (1820); S. Basu, R. Pollack, M.-F. Roy, Algorithms in Real Algebraic Geometry, ch. 2.
  • Example: for p = X² − 1 on (0, 2], the theorem bounds the number of roots (one, at 1) by the drop of sign variations of (X² − 1, 2X, 2) from 0 to 2, which is 1.
