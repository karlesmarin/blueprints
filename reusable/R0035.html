Godsil–Gutman: the sum over all ±1 edge signings of the characteristic polynomials of the signed adjacency matrices is (number of signings) times the matching polynomial.

Lean docstring

Godsil–Gutman identity. Summed over all ±1 signings of the edges, the characteristic polynomials of the signed adjacency matrices give #signings • μ(G), the matching polynomial: the expected characteristic polynomial of a uniformly random signing is μ(G). This is the spectral bridge of the Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava interlacing-families method.

  • Archive: SimpleGraph.godsil_gutman (Formalizations.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatchingPolynomial.GodsilGutman); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: C. D. Godsil, I. Gutman, On the matching polynomial of a graph, Algebraic Methods in Graph Theory (1981) 241–249.
  • Example: for one edge the two signings give X² − 1 twice, and μ(K₂) = X² − 1.
