Lean docstring

Product of sines. For every n ≥ 1, ∏_{k=1}^{n-1} 2 sin(kπ/n) = n. The proof goes through IsPrimitiveRoot.prod_pow_sub_one_eq_order and |e^{2πik/n} − 1| = 2 sin(πk/n).

  • Archive: SpinRhoLine.prod_two_sin (Formalizations.RepresentationTheory.SpinRhoLine, used for the spin values on the ρ-line); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: the identity ∏_{k=1}^{n-1} sin(kπ/n) = n / 2^{n-1} (folklore; it is the value at 1 of (Xⁿ − 1)/(X − 1) = ∏ (X − ζᵏ)).
  • Example: prod_two_sin_mul_pi_div 4 (by norm_num) gives (2 sin(π/4)) (2 sin(2π/4)) (2 sin(3π/4)) = 4.
