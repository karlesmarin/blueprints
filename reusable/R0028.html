Lean docstring

The neighbourhood function of a set family is submodular. For an indexed family of finite sets t : ι → Finset α and s u : Finset ι, with N(s) = s.biUnion t (the union of the t i, i ∈ s): |N(s ∪ u)| + |N(s ∩ u)| ≤ |N(s)| + |N(u)|. It is the inequality behind the deficiency form of Hall's theorem (Kőnig–Ore) and behind the transversal matroid.

  • Archive: Finset.card_biUnion_union_add_card_biUnion_inter_le (Formalizations.Combinatorics.Hall.Tight, which imports only Mathlib.Combinatorics.Hall.Finite); the module cites no paper DOI of ours. Mathlib proves Hall's theorem by another induction and has no tight-set lemmas.
  • Classical reference: L. Lovász, M. D. Plummer, Matching Theory (1986), ch. 1.
  • Example: for t i = {i, i + 1} on ℕ, s = {0}, u = {1}, it reads 3 + 0 ≤ 2 + 2.
