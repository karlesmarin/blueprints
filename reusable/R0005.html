Lean docstring

Glasser's integral (the modified Bessel K_{1/2} integral). For a ≥ 0 and b > 0, ∫_{v > 0} v^{-1/2} exp(−a/v − b v) dv = √(π/b) · exp(−2 √(a b)). The case a = 0 is the Gamma integral; for a > 0 the proof substitutes v = √(a/b) s² and uses Schlömilch's change of variables u = s − 1/s.

  • Archive: Superstatistics.glasser_integral (Formalizations.Probability.Distributions.Superstatistics.LaplaceMixture, where it gives the Laplace law as a Gaussian scale mixture); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: M. L. Glasser, A remarkable property of definite integrals, Math. Comp. 40 (1983) 561; D. F. Andrews, C. L. Mallows, J. Roy. Statist. Soc. Ser. B 36 (1974) 99–102.
  • Example: glasser_integral le_rfl one_pos is ∫ v^{-1/2} e^{-v} = √π (the Gamma value Γ(1/2)), after simplification.
