Pappus's hexagon theorem

Lean docstring

Pappus's hexagon theorem (homogeneous coordinates, any commutative ring).

Points of the projective plane are vectors of R³; three points are collinear when their triple product A ⬝ᵥ (B ⨯₃ C) vanishes, and the meet of the lines PQ and RS is (P ⨯₃ Q) ⨯₃ (R ⨯₃ S). If A, B, C are collinear and a, b, c are collinear, then the three meets Ab ∩ aB, Ac ∩ aC, Bc ∩ bC are collinear. No non-degeneracy hypothesis is needed: the conclusion is an ideal-membership identity.

  • Archive: Pappus.pappus (Formalizations.Geometry.Projective.Pappus); no research paper.
  • Classical reference: Pappus of Alexandria, Collection, Book VII (c. 320 AD); R. Whitty, Theorem of the Day, «Pappus' Theorem».
  • Example: over ℤ, from h₁ : A ⬝ᵥ (B ⨯₃ C) = 0 and h₂ : a ⬝ᵥ (b ⨯₃ c) = 0, pappus_hexagon A B C a b c h₁ h₂ gives the collinearity of the three meets.
