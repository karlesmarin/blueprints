Lean docstring

Bondy's theorem on induced subsets. Let X be a nonempty finite set and F a family of at most |X| distinct finite sets. Then some x ∈ X can be deleted from every member of F without merging two of them: A ↦ A.erase x is injective on F. The sets need not be subsets of X. Typical uses: test covers and separating systems, and deleting a coordinate of a binary code without identifying two codewords.

  • Archive: Bondy.exists_erase_injOn (Formalizations.Combinatorics.SetFamily.Bondy, proved by the linear-algebra argument over ZMod 2); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: J. A. Bondy, Induced subsets, J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 12 (1972) 201–202.
  • Example: for X = {1, 2} and F = {∅, {1}}, deleting 2 keeps the two sets distinct.
