Over a field, the rank of the Kronecker product A ⊗ₖ B of two matrices is rank A · rank B.

Lean docstring

The rank of a Kronecker product is the product of the ranks. Over a field K, for A : Matrix m n K and B : Matrix p q K, rank (A ⊗ₖ B) = rank A · rank B. The proof identifies A ⊗ₖ B with the tensor product of the linear maps of A and B and uses that vector spaces are flat.

  • Archive: Matrix.rank_kronecker (Formalizations.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.Rank.Kronecker, used by the free-locus certificates and the orbifold products of the same archive); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: R. A. Horn, C. R. Johnson, Topics in Matrix Analysis (1991), ch. 4.
  • Example: the adjacency matrix of a tensor product of graphs is a Kronecker product, so its rank is the product of the ranks of the factors.
