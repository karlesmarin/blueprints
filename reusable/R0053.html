Lean docstring

Newton's identity for traces. For a square matrix M over a commutative ring R, with charpolyRev M = det (1 − X • M) viewed in R⟦X⟧, d/dX (charpolyRev M) = − charpolyRev M · ∑ₖ trace (Mᵏ⁺¹) Xᵏ: the logarithmic derivative of the reversed characteristic polynomial is minus the generating function of the traces of the powers of M. No eigenvalues and no hypothesis on R.

  • Archive: Matrix.derivative_coe_charpolyRev (Formalizations.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.Charpoly.Jacobi: Jacobi's formula plus the resolvent series (1 − X • M)⁻¹ = ∑ₖ Mᵏ Xᵏ); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: Newton's identities between power sums and elementary symmetric functions; no source verified (unverified).
  • Example: the identity for a 2 × 2 integer matrix.

Upstream
