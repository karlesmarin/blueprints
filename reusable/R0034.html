Over a field, a block matrix with invertible top-left block A has rank card m + rank (D - C A⁻¹ B); with A positive definite and zero Schur complement it is PSD of rank card m.

Lean docstring

Rank through the Schur complement. Over a field, if the top-left block A of a block matrix is invertible, then rank (fromBlocks A B C D) = card m + rank (D - C * A⁻¹ * B). With A ≻ 0 and a zero Schur complement this is the exact certificate "this PSD Gram matrix has rank card m" (Matrix.posSemidef_rank_of_schur_eq_zero in the archive). Mathlib has the PSD half (Matrix.PosDef.fromBlocks₁₁) and the LDU factorization, not the rank.

  • Archive: Matrix.rank_fromBlocks_of_isUnit₁₁ (Formalizations.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.SchurRank; also Matrix.rank_fromBlocks_diagonal); a piece of the design table (Schur certificates of sums of squares); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: textbook (Guttman rank additivity; F. Zhang, The Schur Complement and Its Applications, Springer 2005, §0.9).
  • Example: !![1, 1; 1, 1] has A = 1 and Schur complement 1 - 1 * 1 * 1 = 0, so rank 1.
