example (p : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] : ((p.bell : ℕ) : ZMod p) = 2 := by

Lean docstring

Touchard's congruence (1933). For a prime p and every n, the Bell numbers (Mathlib's Nat.bell) satisfy B(n + p) = B(n) + B(n + 1) in ZMod p; in particular B(p) ≡ 2 (mod p). The proof uses the umbral functional L(Xⁿ) = B(n) on (ZMod p)[X], for which the falling factorial of order p is X^p − X.

  • Archive: Touchard.bell_add_prime (Formalizations.Combinatorics.Enumerative.Touchard); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: J. Touchard, Propriétés arithmétiques de certains nombres récurrents, Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles A 53 (1933) 21–31.
  • Example: B(5) = 52 ≡ 2 (mod 5).
