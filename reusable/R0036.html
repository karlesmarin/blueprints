theorem bass_determinant_commRing {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] (G : SimpleGraph V)
    [DecidableRel G.Adj] (R : Type*) [CommRing R] (u : R) :
    (1 - u ^ 2) ^ (Fintype.card V) * (1 - u • G.hashimoto R).det
      = (1 - u ^ 2) ^ G.edgeFinset.card
        * (1 - u • G.adjMatrix R + u ^ 2 • (G.degMatrix R - 1)).det

Lean docstring

Bass's determinant formula over any commutative ring. For a finite simple graph with Hashimoto (non-backtracking) matrix B, adjacency matrix A and degree matrix D, (1 - u²)^|V| · det (1 - u B) = (1 - u²)^|E| · det (1 - u A + u² (D - 1)) for every u in any commutative ring: the Ihara zeta function in determinant form, with no field and no vertex order.

  • Archive: SimpleGraph.bass_determinant_commRing (Formalizations.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.Ihara.BassCommRing, from the field case bass_charpolyRev); the module cites no paper DOI of ours.
  • Classical reference: H. Bass, The Ihara–Selberg zeta function of a tree lattice, Internat. J. Math. 3 (1992) 717–797.
  • Example: u = 0 gives 1 = 1.
