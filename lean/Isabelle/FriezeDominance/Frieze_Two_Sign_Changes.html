(*  Title:      Frieze_Two_Sign_Changes.thy    Author:     Carles Marín     Two integer friezes of width 12 with two sign changes     Two integer Conway-Coxeter frieze patterns of width 12 (quiddities of length 15) whose rows 6 differ, cyclically,    with no zero and exactly two sign changes. Row 6 = [12/2] lies in the range 1 <= k <= [w/2] of Problem 1 of    Tabachnikov, which asks for which k the difference must change sign at least four times. All facts are finite    computations proved with code_simp. Found by an exhaustive search over all 742 900 friezes of width 12; width 12 is    the smallest width with such a pair in the range k <= [w/2].     Main results:    * exists_two_sign_changes_width_12.     References:    * S. Tabachnikov, A four-vertex theorem for frieze patterns?, Math.
