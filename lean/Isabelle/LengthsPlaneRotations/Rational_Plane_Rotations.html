A "plane of type lam" is a pair (f, g) with   f.g = 0, f.f = a > 0 and g.g = delta a.   Proved without any assumption other than the hypotheses of each statement:   (1) plane_rotation: on the plane, R f = lam f + g, R g = - delta f + lam g (extended linearly) preserves the       scalar product, its adjoint is R* f = lam f - g, R* g = delta f + lam g, and R + R* = 2 lam on the plane.
