(1) The cost of cancelling the leakage (corollary on the robust cost of the robust-offset paper): with the        excess exposures x = A - A0 and y = B - A0, the disk x y >= 1/(4 G^2) gives the branch sqrt(gq gm)/G        (Memory_Robust.robust_cost_amgm) and the duration x + y >= pi/(2 G) gives the branch (pi/2) min(gq, gm)/G;        together, gq x + gm y >= max of the two. (2) The envelopes at a fixed duration (corollary of the fixed-duration theorem of the memory-floors paper):        if shifting the relaxation rates by mu multiplies every population by exp (mu t), the bound of the shifted        system, exp (mu T) p(T) <= max_{s <= T} exp (mu s) p_res(s), is the envelope        p(T) <= exp (-mu T) max_{s <= T} exp (mu s) p_res(s).
