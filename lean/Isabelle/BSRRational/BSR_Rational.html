License:    BSD*) theory BSR_Rational  imports Complex_Main "HOL-Library.Function_Algebras"begin text \<open>Orthogonality of the coefficient vectors for a $C_4$-free support  (Theorem \texttt{thm:bsrq} of \texttt{lengths.tex}, first half of the proof).   A representation of $f=\sum_{(i,j)\in E}c_{ij}x_i^2y_j^2$ as a sum of $r$ squares of bilinear forms  $s_k=\sum_{ij}v_{ij}(k)\,x_iy_j$ has coefficient vectors $v_{ij}\in\mathbb{Q}^r$. Comparing the  coefficients of the monomial $x_ix_ky_jy_l$ in $\sum_k s_k^2=f$ gives the polarized identity  $v_{ij}\cdot v_{kl}+v_{il}\cdot v_{kj}=2\,[i=k\wedge j=l]\,c_{ij}$ (Zhang--Zhu, arXiv:2605.00590,  Prop.~2.1, for general coefficients, with $c=0$ off $E$). This is the hypothesis of the locale.  Nothing is assumed about the field beyond ordered: the vectors are over \<^typ>\<open>rat\<close> and the  inner product is the standard one, so positivity of $v\cdot v$ gives the support lemma.
