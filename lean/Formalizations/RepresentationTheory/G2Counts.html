For lambda = a w1 + b w2, the sixvalues (lambda+rho, alpha) are u, v, u+v, 2u+v, 3u+v, 3u+2v with u = a+1, v = 3(b+1); N_q countsthose divisible by q and r_q the same count on 1,3,4,5,6,9. We prove N_3 = r_3 iff a is not 2 mod 3and N_2 = r_2 iff a, b are not both odd, together with the strict forms N_q > r_q. Theidentification of "character vanishes at a_P^k" with "N_q > r_q" is the paper's criterion and is NOTformalized; no Lie theory is used. ## Main results * `KostantAlphabet.G2Counts.N_three_eq_iff`: N_3 = r_3 iff a is not 2 mod 3* `KostantAlphabet.G2Counts.N_two_eq_iff`: N_2 = r_2 iff a, b are not both odd* `KostantAlphabet.G2Counts.N_three_gt_iff`: r_3 < N_3 iff a = 2 mod 3* `KostantAlphabet.G2Counts.N_two_gt_iff`: r_2 < N_2 iff a, b both odd ## References * C.
