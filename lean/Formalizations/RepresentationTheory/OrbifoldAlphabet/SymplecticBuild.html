Three constructions: * real type: `R = ρ ⊗ I₂` and `J = B ⊗ ε`, with `ρᵀ B ρ = B`, `B` symmetric and `ε` antisymmetric;* complex type: `R = ρ ⊕ ρ̄` and `J = [[0, I], [−I, 0]]`, with `ρ` unitary;* quaternionic type: `R = ρ`, `J = B` (nothing to prove). ## Main results * `kronecker_one_preserves`: `(ρ ⊗ I)ᵀ (B ⊗ ε) (ρ ⊗ I) = (ρᵀ B ρ) ⊗ ε = B ⊗ ε`.* `kronecker_transpose_eq_neg`: a symmetric `B` against an antisymmetric `ε` gives `(B ⊗ ε)ᵀ = −(B ⊗ ε)`.* `fromBlocks_conj_preserves_J`: for unitary `ρ`, `ρ ⊕ ρ̄` preserves `J`; the off-diagonal blocks are  `ρᵀ ρ̄ = (ρᴴ ρ)ᵀ = I` and `ρ̄ᵀ ρ = ρᴴ ρ = I`. ## References * C.
