On thecocharacters `(m, n)` of `U(2)` the relevant quadratic form is`(2 b₀ (m − n)² + b₁ (m + n)²) / 8`. The translation from Lie theory to this quadratic numeratoris an input; nothing is stated about a complete supergravity spectrum. ## Main results * `joint_criterion`: the form is integer-valued iff `2 b₀ + b₁ ≡ 0 (mod 8)`.* `separate_sufficient`: `4 ∣ b₀` and `8 ∣ b₁` together are sufficient.* `positive_joint`, `positive_not_separate`, `joint_does_not_imply_separate`: `(b₀, b₁) = (1, 6)`  is integer-valued, with `(1 / 8)(2 (m − n)² + 6 (m + n)²) = m² + mn + n²`, but  `4 ∤ b₀`; so the separate conditions are not necessary.* `rational_joint_criterion`, `two_samples_suffice`: with rational coefficients `b₀ = p/t`,  `b₁ = q/t`, integrality is decided by the two samples `(1, 0)` and `(1, 1)`. ## References * S. Monnier, G. W. Moore, D. S. Park, *Quantization of anomaly coefficients in 6D  `𝒩 = (1, 0)` supergravity*, JHEP 02 (2018) 020, arXiv:1711.04777, §3.-/ @[expose] public section namespace AnomalyQuantization.U2Example /-- The numerator `2 b₀ (m − n)² + b₁ (m + n)²`. -/def numerator (b0 b1 m n : Int) : Int :=  2 * b0 * (m - n)^2 + b1 * (m + n)^2 /-- The quadratic form is integer-valued. -/def jointIntegral (b0 b1 : Int) : Prop :=  ∀ m n : Int, ∃ k : Int, numerator b0 b1 m n = 8 * k /-- The separate conditions `4 ∣ b₀`, `8 ∣ b₁`. -/def separateIntegral (b0 b1 : Int) : Prop :=  (∃ k : Int, b0 = 4 * k) ∧ (∃ k : Int, b1 = 8 * k) /-- For `(b₀, b₁) = (1, 6)` the form is `m² + mn + n²`. -/theorem positive_identity (m n : Int) :    numerator 1 6 m n = 8 * (m*m + m*n + n*n) := by  unfold numerator  grind /-- `(1, 6)` is integer-valued. -/theorem positive_joint : jointIntegral 1 6 := by  intro m n  exact ⟨m*m + m*n + n*n, positive_identity m n⟩ /-- `(1, 6)` does not satisfy the separate conditions. -/theorem positive_not_separate : ¬ separateIntegral 1 6 := by  intro ⟨⟨k, hk⟩, _⟩  omega /-- The form is integer-valued iff `2 b₀ + b₁ ≡ 0 (mod 8)`. -/theorem joint_criterion (b0 b1 : Int) :    jointIntegral b0 b1 ↔ ∃ d : Int, 2*b0 + b1 = 8*d := by  constructor  · intro h    obtain ⟨d, hd⟩ := h 1 0    exact ⟨d, by simpa [numerator] using hd⟩  · rintro ⟨d, hd⟩ m n    refine ⟨d*(m*m+n*n) + (2*d-b0)*m*n, ?_⟩    unfold numerator    grind /-- The separate conditions are sufficient. -/theorem separate_sufficient (b0 b1 : Int) :    separateIntegral b0 b1 → jointIntegral b0 b1 := by  rintro ⟨⟨k, hk⟩, ⟨l, hl⟩⟩  apply (joint_criterion b0 b1).mpr  exact ⟨k+l, by omega⟩ /-- The separate conditions are not necessary. -/theorem joint_does_not_imply_separate :    ¬ (∀ b0 b1 : Int, jointIntegral b0 b1 → separateIntegral b0 b1) := by  intro h  exact positive_not_separate (h 1 6 positive_joint) /-- Rational coefficients `b₀ = p/t`, `b₁ = q/t` with a common denominator `t ≠ 0`: the form isinteger-valued iff `numerator p q m n ∈ 8 t ℤ`. (Valid also for `t = 0`, which does not encoderationals.) -/def scaledJoint (p q t : Int) : Prop :=  ∀ m n : Int, ∃ k : Int, numerator p q m n = 8*t*k /-- The criterion with rational coefficients. -/theorem rational_joint_criterion (p q t : Int) :    scaledJoint p q t ↔      ∃ c d : Int, p = t*c ∧ 2*p+q = 8*t*d := by  constructor  · intro h    obtain ⟨d, hd⟩ := h 1 0    obtain ⟨e, he⟩ := h 1 1    simp [numerator] at hd he    refine ⟨4*d-e, d, ?_, ?_⟩    · grind    · grind  · rintro ⟨c, d, hc, hd⟩ m n    refine ⟨d*(m*m+n*n) + (2*d-c)*m*n, ?_⟩    unfold numerator    grind /-- Two samples, at `(1, 0)` and `(1, 1)`, decide integrality in this family. -/theorem two_samples_suffice (p q t : Int)    (h10 : ∃ d : Int, numerator p q 1 0 = 8*t*d)    (h11 : ∃ e : Int, numerator p q 1 1 = 8*t*e) :    scaledJoint p q t := by  obtain ⟨d, hd⟩ := h10  obtain ⟨e, he⟩ := h11  simp [numerator] at hd he  apply (rational_joint_criterion p q t).mpr  refine ⟨4*d-e, d, ?_, ?_⟩ <;> grind end AnomalyQuantization.U2Example
