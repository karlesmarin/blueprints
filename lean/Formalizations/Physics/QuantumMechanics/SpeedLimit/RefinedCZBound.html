Two transmons truncated to three levels are coupled by the exchange interaction`H_c = J (a† ⊗ a + a ⊗ a†)` (here `J = 1`). With ideal, instantaneous local control, the operatornorm `‖H_c‖` gives only the crude controlled-Z time bound `T ≥ π / (4 J ‖H_c‖)`-type estimates.The sharper argument compares the true evolution `U(t)` with the evolution `L(t)` that has the samelocal controls but no `H_c`, on the reference-extended product state `Ω₀` (a maximally entangledstate between a rank-two code in `ℂ³` and a two-level passive reference, on each side). TheFubini–Study speed of `U†L Ω₀` is the spread of `H_c` in `L(t) Ω₀`, a state whose physical reducedstate is `(P_A ⊗ P_B) / 4` with `P_A, P_B` rank-two projectors of `ℂ³`. The key finite-dimensionalfact is then   `(1/4) Tr[(P_A ⊗ P_B) H_c²] ≤ 97/32`, with equality for `P = 1 - |v⟩⟨v|`, `v = √(7/8) |0⟩ + √(1/8) |2⟩`. Combined with the fact that`L(T) Ω₀` is a product across the two sides while the CZ state is at Fubini–Study distance `≥ π/4`from every product, this gives `T·√(97/32) ≥ π/4 - arccos(1 - e)` for CZ error `e`. The argument was suggested by an AI reviewer (ChatGPT, OpenAI); the lemma was checked numerically(`research/qsl-hardware/cota_cz_refinada.py` in the Curiosity workspace) and is formalized here. ## Main results (all proved here, sorry-free) * `QSL.Exchange.exchC_sq_apply`: the entries of `H_c²` (a `9 × 9` integer matrix, block diagonal by  total excitation number; the only off-diagonal entries are `⟨20|H_c²|02⟩ = ⟨02|H_c²|20⟩ = 2`).* `QSL.Exchange.trace_expand`: closed form of `Tr[(P_A ⊗ P_B) H_c²]` for `P = 1 - |v⟩⟨v|`, as a  polynomial in the populations `|v_i|², |w_i|²` plus the cross term `2 (z + z̄)`,  `z = v₀ w₂ v̄₂ w̄₀`. No normalization is used.* `QSL.Exchange.refined_cz_lemma`: for unit `v, w ∈ ℂ³`,  `(1/4) Re Tr[(P_v ⊗ P_w) H_c²] ≤ 97/32`. Route: coordinate expansion, AM–GM on the cross term  (`re_crossZ_le`), then the scalar inequality `scalar_ineq`  (`97/8 - G = 2 (p₂ + q₂ - 1/4)² + p₁(…) + q₁(…) ≥ 0`, i.e. completing the square).* `QSL.Exchange.refined_cz_equality`: equality at `v = w = √(7/8) |0⟩ + √(1/8) |2⟩`. ## Not formalized The time bound itself (P2): `sAngle (U Ω₀) (L Ω₀) ≤ √(97/32) Σ τ_k` and the corollary`T √(97/32) ≥ π/4 - arccos(1 - e)`. Plan, reusing `QSL.Qudit` and `QSL.Gate`: 1. Show `‖(H_c ⊗ 1) (U_A ⊗ U_B ⊗ 1) Ω₀‖² = (1/4) Tr[(P_A ⊗ P_B) H_c²]`, where   `Ω₀ = Φ_{AA'} ⊗ Φ_{BB'}`, `Φ = 2^{-1/2} Σ_{a'} |c_{a'}⟩ ⊗ |a'⟩` (`c_0, c_1` an orthonormal pair   in `ℂ³`), `U_A, U_B` unitary, and `P_A = Σ |U_A c_a'⟩⟨U_A c_a'|`.2. Show every rank-two projector of `ℂ³` has the form `1 - |v⟩⟨v|` with `v` a unit vector (the   conjugate cross product of the code pair), so `refined_cz_lemma` applies.3.
