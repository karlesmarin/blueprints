We measure distances betweenunit vectors with the Fubini–Study angle `arccos ‖⟪u, v⟫‖`, which satisfies the triangleinequality and is invariant under unitaries. A Duhamel estimate, split into `N` equal pieces andletting `N → ∞`, shows that the angle between `exp (X + Y) ψ` and `exp X ψ` is at most `‖Y‖` forskew-Hermitian `X, Y`. Chaining over the steps of the control, the final state of an evolutionstarted at a product vector lies within angle `J · ∑ τₖ` of a product vector. Hence, if every unitproduct vector has overlap at most `λ` with the target state, `arccos λ ≤ J · ∑ τₖ`. For thetwo-level Bell vector `(e₀₀ + e₁₁)/√2` inside qudits of any dimension `d ≥ 2`, `λ = 1/√2`, so thetotal time is at least `π / (4 J)`; this is exact (attained by `J · Z ⊗ X` for qubits).
