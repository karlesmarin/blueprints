The geometric realization ofthe period triples and the evenness of the intersection pairing are inputs, not formalized. `t`is a common denominator in applications (nonzero); the statements remain valid as divisibilitystatements at `t = 0`. ## Main results * `universal_basis`, `spin_basis`: the periods lie in `t ℤ` iff `t ∣ p, q, r`, respectively  `t ∣ p, q, 2r`.* `universal_implies_spin`, `half_square_spin`, `half_square_not_universal`: the spin condition  is strictly weaker.* `q6_congruence`, `q6_implies_six`: for the `q = 6` row of Table 1 of Choi–Forslund–Lam–Shao  (with `A = c₂(E₂)`, `B = c₂(E₃)`, `C = c₁(E₃)²` and mathematical sign conventions for Chern  classes) the spin condition is `18 K₂ + 24 K₃ + K₁ ≡ 0 (mod 36)`, and it forces `6 ∣ K₁`. ## References * Y. Choi, M. Forslund, H. T. Lam, S.-H. Shao, *Quantization of axion-gauge couplings and  non-invertible higher symmetries*, Phys. Rev.
