The `δκₙ` are exact rational functions of`α`; they are inputs here (derived by computer algebra), and the file checks the identitiesbetween them as identities in the field `ℝ`. The catalog terminates at `n = 12`: the Standard Model potential in the old field is of degree`12` in `h`, so `δκ₁₃ = 0`; and `δκ₁₁ = δκ₁₂`. ## Main results * `ring_identity_four`: `δκ₄ − 6 δκ₃ = α (3α³ + 30α² + 35α − 4) / (3 (α + 1)²)`.* `ring_identity_five`: `δκ₅ − 15 δκ₃ = 5α (12α³ + 75α² + 68α − 3) / (3 (α + 1)²)`.* `ring_identity_six`: `δκ₆ − 15 δκ₃ = 5α (α + 3)(132α² + 99α − 1) / (3 (α + 1)²)`.* `ring_identity_four_leading_order`: `δκ₄ − 6 δκ₃ = −(4/3) α + O(α²)`, with the remainder  written out.* `dim8_break_four`, `dim8_break_five`, `dim8_break_six`: at first order in `α` and in the  dimension-8 parameter `β = v⁴ (C_{HD8} + C_{H,D2}) / Λ⁴` the three identities acquire the  terms `−(3/2) β`, `−(125/8) β`, `−(573/8) β`.* `dk7_factored`, …, `dk10_factored`, `dk3_factored`, …, `dk6_factored`: expanded and  factored forms of each closed form agree; `dk11_eq_dk12`: `δκ₁₁ = δκ₁₂`. ## Related `SMEFT.HiggsSelfCouplings` treats the leading order with the `c_φ` pieces kept separate; thisfile has the all-orders closed forms in `α`, in which the `c_φ` pieces are absent. At leadingorder both give `δκ₄ − 6 δκ₃ = −(4/3) α`. The leading-order identities are consistent with thegeometric SMEFT of Helset, Martin and Trott and are not claimed as new. ## References * L.
