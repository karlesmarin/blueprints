A weight records the Wilson-line charge `q` and two orbifold parities `p`, `b`; the`7`, `Sym² 7 = 28`, `End 7 = 48 + 1` and `Sym³ 7 = 84` are built as lists of weights, and theircharge channels give the gauge-sector coefficients and the moment generators `(8D, 2A₄)` of theeight bulk multiplets.
