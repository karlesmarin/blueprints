A component of abulk multiplet survives in the multiplet of parities `(η, η')` iff `P₅ P₅' = η η'`, and thencarries `4`D chirality `−η P₅` (Komori–Maru, eqs. (37)–(40)). The component `(i, j̄)` of`n ⊗ n̄` carries the parity `p i * (p j)⁻¹`; for its conjugate `(j, ī)` this is `p j * (p i)⁻¹`.When every `p i` squares to `1` the two coincide, so `(i, j̄)` and `(j, ī)` survive together, withthe same chirality and opposite charges: the surviving spectrum of the adjoint is vector-like. The hypothesis `p i * p i = 1` is exactly what is used: in a general commutative group theconjugate index carries the inverse parity and the swap no longer preserves it. For the `ℤ₂`parities of the model (`ℤˣ`-valued) it holds automatically (`Int.units_mul_self`). The counts of the paper are checked by `decide` on its data`P₅ = diag(+,+,+,+,+,−,−)`, `P₅' = diag(+,+,+,−,−,−,+)`, with the hypercharge and the extra`U(1)` per index (in units of `1/2`). Off the Cartan (`42` components, as in the paper'sscript), a `48^(+,+)` has `18` survivors and a `48^(+,−)` has `24`, all paired; the complexcontrols `28 = Sym² 7` and `84 = Sym³ 7` leave `10` and `34` unpaired states at `(+,+)`."Paired" means: another survivor of the same chirality with opposite `(Y, extra)`. ## Main results * `adjParity_swap`: with parities squaring to `1`, `(i, j̄)` and `(j, ī)` have the same parity.* `survives_swap`, `chirality_swap`, `charge_swap`: the swap preserves survival and chirality and  negates every additive charge.* `adjoint_vectorLike`: every off-diagonal survivor of the adjoint has a distinct partner of the  same chirality and opposite charges, for any `n`, any `ℤˣ` parities and any `(η, η')`.* `adjoint_survivors_pp`, `adjoint_survivors_pm`: `18` and `24` survivors on the paper's data;  `adjoint_unpaired_pp`, `adjoint_unpaired_pm`: none unpaired (derived from `adjoint_vectorLike`).* `sym2_survivors`, `sym2_unpaired`, `sym3_survivors`, `sym3_unpaired`: the controls,  `16` survivors with `10` unpaired, and `44` survivors with `34` unpaired. ## References * C.
