Boundary conditions related by a gauge transformation that shifts theWilson line are equivalent, and the equivalence is generated by the move`[p; q, r; s] ~ [p−1; q+1, r+1; s−1]`. The winding element `U = P₀ P₁` has`det U = (−1)^{q+r}`, so the theory has a reflection-coset sector (where the boundary sign canact) exactly when `q + r` is odd. This file counts the classes that have one, for every `N`. ## Main results * `label_move`, `rank_move`, `label_eqv`, `rank_eqv`: the label `(p+q, q+s)` and `N` are  invariant.* `eqv_of_label`: two boundary conditions with the same `N` and the same label are joined by a  chain of moves, so the label is a complete invariant; with `witness_label` the classes are the  pairs `(u, v)` with `u, v ≤ N`, and `card_classes` recovers the count `(N+1)²`.* `coset_parity`: `q + r` and `u + v + N` have the same parity, so having a coset sector is a  property of the class.* `card_cosetClasses`: exactly `⌊(N+1)²/2⌋` of the `(N+1)²` classes have a coset sector. ## References * N. Haba, Y. Hosotani, Y. Kawamura, *Classification and dynamics of equivalence classes in  SU(N) gauge theory on the orbifold S¹/Z₂*, arXiv:hep-ph/0309088.* C.
