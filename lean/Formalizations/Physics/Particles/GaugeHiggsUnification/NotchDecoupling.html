It is linear in `δ` and vanishes when `δ(m) = 0` at every even `m` (the notch),so the fermion sector drops out and `𝒟` is the gauge part alone. On `range (2N)` it is the plainalternating weight `−4π² ∑_{m' < N} (−1)^{m'} m'² δ(2m')`.
