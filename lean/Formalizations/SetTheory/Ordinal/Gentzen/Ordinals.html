These statements go through `NONote.repr` and Mathlib's order on `NONote`, which is defined by`repr`, so they depend on `Classical.choice` (through `Ordinal.lt_wf`). The choice-free versions,stated with a syntactic order and normal form, are in`Formalizations.SetTheory.Ordinal.Gentzen.Acc` and `Formalizations.SetTheory.Ordinal.Gentzen.TwoPow`. ## Main results * `twoPow`, `repr_twoPow`: `2 ^ a` as a normal notation;* `twoPow_lt_twoPow`, `twoPow_le_twoPow` (L3), `twoPow_max` (L4);* `L1` (`1 ≤ a → 2^a + 1 < 2^(a+1)`) and `L1_fails_at_zero`;* `L2` (`2 ≤ a → 2^a + 2 < 2^(a+1)`) and `L2_fails_at_one`;* `L5` (`2^a + 2 < 2^(a+2)`), `twoPow_add_le_max` (`2^a + 2^b ≤ 2^(max a b + 1)`);* `ti`: transfinite induction below `ε₀`, from `NONote.lt_wf`. ## References * B.
