The proof is byuniqueness of antiderivatives (`PowerSeries.derivative.ext`): no topology and no `exp`. ## Main results * `PowerSeries.deriv_logOf_mul`: `(logOf h)' · h = h'` for `h` with constant coefficient `1`.* `PowerSeries.logOf_mul`: `logOf (f * g) = logOf f + logOf g`.* `PowerSeries.logOf_one`, `PowerSeries.logOf_pow`: `logOf 1 = 0`, `logOf (f ^ n) = n • logOf f`.* `PowerSeries.coeff_logTrunc_pow`, `PowerSeries.polyPL_coeff`: the coefficient form of  `logOf ((1 + X) ^ p) = p • log(1 + X)` for the truncated logarithm, as a power series and as a  polynomial. This is the form used for the Adams eigen-relation of the Connes–Kreimer Hopf algebra  (`Formalizations.RingTheory.HopfAlgebra.ConnesKreimer.Eulerian`). Mathlib has `logOf`, `derivative_log_mul_one_add_X` (`(log(1 + X))' · (1 + X) = 1`, used here) and`logOf_exp`, but at the pinned commit not the additivity `logOf_mul`. ## References * C. Marín, *How a Tree Forgets Its Order: the Eulerian idempotent and the Adams spectrum on the  commutative Connes–Kreimer–Butcher Hopf algebra of rooted trees, machine-checked in Lean 4*  (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.20774821.
