Equality is of multisets with multiplicities. The ambient field is arbitrary (the paper worksover C); no characteristic hypothesis is needed. The additive forms A + 2{1} = d.mu_q and A + {1} +{-1} = d.mu_q are also proved. Nothing about Lie theory or characters is formalized. ## Main results * `KostantAlphabet.alphabet_eq_even`: A = d.mu_q - 2.{1} for d even* `KostantAlphabet.alphabet_eq_odd`: A = d.mu_q - {1} - {-1} for d odd* `KostantAlphabet.alphabet_add_even`: additive form, d even* `KostantAlphabet.alphabet_add_odd`: additive form, d odd ## References * C.
