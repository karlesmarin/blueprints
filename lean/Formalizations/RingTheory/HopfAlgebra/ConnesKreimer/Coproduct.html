Coassociativityholds up to `List.Perm` (equality of formal sums). ## Main results * `ConnesKreimer.coprodTree`, `ConnesKreimer.coprodForest`: the coproduct on trees and forests.* `ConnesKreimer.tmul_assoc`: the leg-wise forest product on formal tensors is associative.* `ConnesKreimer.coprodForest_append`: `Δ` is multiplicative (combinatorially).* `ConnesKreimer.coprod_coassoc`: `(Δ ⊗ id) Δ t` and `(id ⊗ Δ) Δ t` agree up to permutation. ## Implementation notes Mathlib's `RootedTree` (`Mathlib.Order.SuccPred.Tree`) is a rooted tree given by its ancestorshiporder on a carrier type. It is not used here: the Hopf algebra needs a type whose elements are the(isomorphism classes of) finite planar trees, to serve as a basis, and an inductive type gives thatwith decidable, computable cuts.
