This file proves the counts the paper uses and the arithmetic that decides it. ## Main results * `marginal_injective`, `card_labels`: distinct labels have distinct readouts, and there are `4^k` labels, so the  semigroup has `n = 4^k` generators.* `sum_choose_mul_two_pow`: `∑ C(k, j) 2^j = 3^k`, the number of faces of the boundary complex of the cross-polytope,  which is `dim S`.* `fms_bound_met`, `fms_bound_violated`: `4^k = 2·3^k − 2` for `k = 1, 2` and `4^k > 2·3^k − 2` for `k ≥ 3`.* `not_completeIntersection_of_fms`: Proposition 2 of the paper, with the cited Corollary 3.4 of Fischer, Morris and  Shapiro (a complete intersection of dimension `d ≥ 2` has at most `2d − 2` extreme rays) as a hypothesis.* `cut_vertices`, `edges_exceed`: the count showing that `(S¹/ℤ₂)^k` is not a cut ideal for `k ≥ 3` (equation (5)).* `not_cone_of_others`, `generator_extreme`: a one-hot block vector of a family of distinct choices is not a  non-negative combination of the others, so every generator of `(S¹/ℤ₂)^k` spans an extreme ray of the cone (the  first step of the proof of Proposition 2).* `rank_circle`, `generators_eq`, `rank_generators`: the generator matrix of `(S¹/ℤ₂)^k` is, up to reindexing, the  `k`-th Kronecker power of a `4 × 4` matrix of rank `3`, so it has rank `3^k`: `dim S = 3^k` (the rank part of  Proposition 2). ## References * C.
