Over an arbitrary division ring `K` (no commutativity): ## Main results * `fromBlocks_schur_mul`, `fromBlocks_schur_mul'`: two-sided block inverse of  `fromBlocks A B C (S + C A⁻¹ B)` from two-sided inverses of the pivot `A` and of the Schur  complement `S`, over any ring.* `phiMatrix`, `phiMatrixInv` (Remark 8.2): `M = [[diag d, −ω d₂], [α, w]]` and its explicit  inverse; the Schur complement of `diag d` is `φ d₂` (`phiMatrix_schur`).  `phiMatrix_mul_inv`, `phiMatrixInv_mul`: both products are `1`; `phiMatrix_isUnit`;  `phi_inv_eq_corner`: `φ⁻¹ = d₂ (M⁻¹)_{∞∞}`.* `phi_inv_woodbury` (Remark 8.2, second compression): for `S = diag d + ω (d₂ w⁻¹) α` and a right  inverse `T` of `S`, `φ⁻¹ = c − c (α T ω) c` with `c = d₂ w⁻¹`.* `inv_mul_conj` (Corollary 7.10(c)): `m₀ ℓ⁻¹ m₁ = (m₀ ℓ m₀⁻¹)⁻¹ (m₀ m₁)`.* `harmonic_compression_inv` (Remark 7.11(iii)): the case `r = 0` of  `InverseIdentities.compression_inv`. Matrix inverses are given explicitly and checked on both sides; `Matrix.inv` needs a commutativering. ## Related Over a commutative ring the block inverse is Mathlib's `Matrix.invOf_fromBlocks₁₁_eq` (with`Invertible` instances), and `phi_inv_woodbury` is a scalar reading of the Woodbury identity`Matrix.invOf_add_mul_mul'` (over a ring, with `Invertible` instances).-/ @[expose] public section
