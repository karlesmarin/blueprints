The pencil is an atom when the coefficient matrices `𝖢, 𝖣` generate thefull matrix algebra and `𝖣` and `𝖤 = 𝖣 − 1 − 𝖢²` are invertible. This file proves these threefacts for the two families used in the paper: * `𝖣_d = diag(3, 4, …, d + 2)` and the cyclic permutation matrix `𝖢_d`  (`(𝖢_d)_{i, i+1} = 1`, indices mod `d`), for every `d ≥ 1`, over any field of characteristic `0`;* `𝖣 = diag(λ, λ + 1)` and the swap `𝖢 = 𝖢_2` (the matrices of `F^{(λ)}`), over any field. That a diagonal matrix with distinct eigenvalues and the cyclic permutation matrix generate thefull matrix algebra is a classical fact (Lagrange interpolation gives the diagonal matrix units,and the cycle moves them around); it is proved here only because we did not find it in Mathlib.The invertibility of `𝖤_d` uses Mathlib's Gershgorin theorem (`det_ne_zero_of_sum_row_lt_diag`):`𝖤_d` is strictly diagonally dominant over `ℚ`, and the statement is transferred to any field ofcharacteristic `0` through `Rat.castHom`. The step "these conditions imply that the pencil is an atom" is Cohn's Theorem 5.8.8 (in the formof Helton–Klep–Volčič, Remark 2.6); it is not formalized here. ## Main results * `ThreeAxes.SizeChain.adjoin_cyc_diagonal_eq_top`: if `v` is injective, then  `Algebra.adjoin K {cyc K d, diagonal v} = ⊤`.* `adjoin_cyc_diagD_eq_top`: `𝖢_d` and `𝖣_d` generate `M_d(K)` (characteristic `0`).* `det_diagD_ne_zero`, `det_matE_ne_zero`: `𝖣_d` and `𝖤_d = 𝖣_d − 1 − 𝖢_d²` are invertible  (characteristic `0`).* `adjoin_swap_diagLam_eq_top`: `𝖢_2` and `diag(λ, λ + 1)` generate `M_2(K)`.* `det_diagLam_ne_zero_and_iff`: `diag(λ, λ+1)` and `diag(λ, λ+1) − 1 − 𝖢_2²` are both invertible  iff `λ ∉ {−1, 0, 1, 2}`. ## References * P.
