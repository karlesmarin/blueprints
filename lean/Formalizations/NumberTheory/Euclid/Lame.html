If the algorithm on `(x, y)` with `y > 0` takes `n` steps, then `F_{n+1} ≤ y` (Fibonaccinumbers, `F_1 = F_2 = 1`), with equality at consecutive Fibonacci numbers; since `10^k ≤ F_{5k+2}`,`n` is at most five times the number of decimal digits of `y` (Lamé, 1844). The proof is a stronginduction on `b` with the invariant `F_{n+1} ≤ b` and, when `b < a`, `F_{n+2} ≤ a`. ## Main results * `Lame.fib_euclidSteps_le`: F_(n+1) <= y and n <= 5 * (decimal digits of y), n = Euclid steps on (x, y) ## References * G. Lamé, *Note sur la limite du nombre des divisions dans la recherche du plus grand commun diviseur entre deux nombres entiers*, C. R. Acad. Sci.
