This fileproves the combinatorial half of that statement: the count of pairs in closed form,`#{(a, b) | 2a + 3b = k} = ⌊k/6⌋ + 1 − [k ≡ 1 (mod 6)]`, and that for `k ≥ 3` it equals `1`(i.e. `deg Q_k = 0`) exactly for `k ∈ {3, 4, 5, 7}`. The identification of `deg Q_k + 1` with this count (Newton–Waring with nonvanishingcoefficients) is not formalized here. ## Main results * `P3d.card_degreePairs`: `#{(a, b) | 2a + 3b = k} = k / 6 + 1 − [k % 6 = 1]`, for every `k`.* `P3d.card_degreePairs_eq_one_iff`: for `k ≥ 3`, the count is `1` iff `k ∈ {3, 4, 5, 7}`. ## References * C.
