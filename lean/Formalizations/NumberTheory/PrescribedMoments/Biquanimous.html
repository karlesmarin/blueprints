For `n ≥ 3`,every partition of `n` into exactly four parts is biquanimous iff `n ∈ {3, 4, 6}`. The number ofsign vectors `ε ∈ {±1}^l` with `∑ w_i ε_i = 0`, up to `ε ~ −ε`, is `½ #{A ⊆ [l] | 2 ∑_A w = n}`;it is `0` iff the weights are not biquanimous, and `0` for odd `n`.
