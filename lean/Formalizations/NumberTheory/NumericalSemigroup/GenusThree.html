Every gap is at most `2δ − 1 = 5`(`P3b.gap_le_two_mul_genus_sub_one`), `1` is a gap, sums of non-gaps are not gaps, and a finite case analysis(`decide` over the subsets of `{1,…,5}`) leaves the four sets. ## Main results * `P3a.gaps_of_genus_eq_three`: the four gap sets of genus three. ## References * C. Marín, *The order generated by a column of the SU(n)_k S-matrix is locally a numerical  semigroup ring (Part II)*, Zenodo (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.22864267, Lemma `lem:delta3`.-/ @[expose] public
