Hence if `g` is the `i`-th gap (`i` gaps in `[0, g]`), then`g + 1 ≤ 2i`, i.e. `g_i ≤ 2i − 1`; in particular every gap is at most `2δ − 1`, `δ` the genus. The second part is Corollary `cor:delta2` of the paper, from the generating rule alone: if`S_T = ⟨e, {j < e | M_j ≠ 0}⟩` with `M_1 = 0` (the spectrum is centred) and `e ≥ 4`, then`δ ≥ 2 ↔ M_2 M_3 = 0`. The moments are abstracted to a predicate `V j` ("`M_j` vanishes");the generating rule itself (Part I of the paper) enters as the definition of `genSet`. ## Main results * `P3b.two_mul_card_gaps_ge`: `g ∉ ⟨A⟩ → g + 1 ≤ 2 · #{x ≤ g | x ∉ ⟨A⟩}`.* `P3b.gap_le_two_mul_index_sub_one`: `g_i ≤ 2i − 1` with `i = #{x ≤ g | x ∉ ⟨A⟩}`.* `P3b.gap_le_two_mul_genus_sub_one`: every gap is `≤ 2δ − 1` when the gaps are finite.* `P3b.two_le_genus_iff`: `cor:delta2`, `2 ≤ δ ↔ V 2 ∨ V 3`. ## References * C.
