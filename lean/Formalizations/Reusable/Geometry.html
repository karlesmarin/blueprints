Points of the projective plane are vectors of `R³`; three points are collinear when their tripleproduct `A ⬝ᵥ (B ⨯₃ C)` vanishes, and the meet of the lines `PQ` and `RS` is`(P ⨯₃ Q) ⨯₃ (R ⨯₃ S)`. If `A, B, C` are collinear and `a, b, c` are collinear, then the three meets`Ab ∩ aB`, `Ac ∩ aC`, `Bc ∩ bC` are collinear. No non-degeneracy hypothesis is needed: theconclusion is an ideal-membership identity. * Archive: `Pappus.pappus` (`Formalizations.Geometry.Projective.Pappus`); no research paper.* Classical reference: Pappus of Alexandria, *Collection*, Book VII (c. 320 AD); R.
