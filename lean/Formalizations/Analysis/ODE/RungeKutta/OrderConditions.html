This file builds a computable engine over planar rooted trees (order `|t|`, density`γ(t)`, elementary weight `Φ(t)(A, b)`), states the order conditions in the division-free form`γ(t) · Φ(t) = 1`, reduces "order `p`" to a finite catalogue check, and certifies concrete methods. ## Main results * `Butcher.satisfiesOrderConditions_iff`: "order `p`" holds iff the condition holds on the finite  catalogue of trees with at most `p` nodes (sound and complete).* `Butcher.euler_ord1`, `Butcher.heun_ord2`, `Butcher.heun_not_ord3`: Euler has order 1, Heun  order 2 and not 3.* `Butcher.rk4_order4`, `Butcher.rk4_not_ord5`: the classical RK4 has order 4 and not 5.* `Butcher.dp_order5`: Dormand–Prince (DOPRI5) has order 5.* `Butcher.gauss_order4`: Gauss–Legendre with 3 stages has order 4, coefficients in `ℚ(√15)`.* `Butcher.gauss_order6`: the Gauss–Legendre tableau with 3 stages, cleared of denominators by  `D = 360` into `ℤ[√15]`, satisfies `γ(t) · Φ(D·A, D·b, t) = D^{|t|}` for every tree with at most  6 nodes (by `decide`). The equivalence with the unscaled conditions (homogeneity of `Φ`) is not  formalized.* `Butcher.orderCond_node_perm`: `Φ` and `γ` are invariant under permuting a node's children, so  the conditions on planar trees subsume those on abstract trees. ## Implementation notes Only the algebraic order conditions are certified, not Butcher's theorem that they are equivalentto a local error `O(h^{p+1})`.
