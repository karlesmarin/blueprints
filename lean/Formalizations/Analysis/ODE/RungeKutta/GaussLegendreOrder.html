Thewitness is the bushy tree with seven nodes (root with six leaves), whose order condition is thequadrature condition `∑ bᵢ cᵢ⁶ = 1/7`; a three-node Gauss rule is exact only up to degree 5. ## Main results * `Butcher.gauss_not_ord7`: the unscaled `ℚ(√15)` tableau fails the order condition on the bushy  tree with seven nodes.* `Butcher.gauss_not_order7`: hence `¬ satisfiesOrderConditions gauss_A gauss_b 7`.* `Butcher.gaussI_not_ord7`, `Butcher.gaussI_exact_order6`: the same failure for the scaled integer  tableau of `Butcher.gauss_order6`, and with it "order 6 and not 7" for that tableau.* `Butcher.gaussI_all_ord7_fail`: every planar tree with exactly seven nodes fails the scaled  integer condition (132 planar trees; the paper counts the 48 abstract ones). ## References * E. Hairer, C. Lubich, G. Wanner, *Geometric Numerical Integration*, Springer (2006).* C.
