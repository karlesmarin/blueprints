The collapse uses`s² = 2`: for another constant the numerator and denominator do not factor. The constant `s` iskept abstract (`0 < s`, `s ^ 2 = 2`) and instantiated at `√2` at the end. ## Main results * `gammaPade_eq_mobius`: `γ_Padé = (1 − u) / (1 + u)` (uses `s² = 2`).* `mobius_mobius`: `x ↦ (1 − x) / (1 + x)` is an involution, `(1 − γ) / (1 + γ) = u`.* `dHorizon_eq`: `θ s (1 − γ) / (1 + γ) = T` at `γ = γ_Padé`, so the information horizon  `d_horizon` evaluated at the Padé exponent is the training length `T`.* `beta_mobius`: `−(1 − γ²) / 2 = −2u / (1 + u)²` for `γ = (1 − u) / (1 + u)`.* `gammaPade_sqrt_two_eq_mobius`: the collapse at `s = √2`. ## Related The same exponent appears, as `pade11 (T √2 / θ)`, in `TransformerAttention.Pade`(`pade11_z_substitution`).
