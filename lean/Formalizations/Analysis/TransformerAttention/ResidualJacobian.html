Expanding theproduct gives `J − I = Σ_k w_k′ + (cross terms)`. ## Main results * `step`, `flow`, `jac`: one residual layer, the stream through a list of layers (head first), and the ordered  product of `I + fderiv w` along the trajectory.* `hasFDerivAt_flow`: `HasFDerivAt (flow ws) (jac ws x) x`. ## References * C.
