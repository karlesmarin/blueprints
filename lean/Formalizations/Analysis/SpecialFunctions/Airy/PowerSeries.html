This file defines these coefficients over `ℚ` and proves that the resulting powerseries satisfies `d²/dX² y = X · y` in `ℚ⟦X⟧`. ## Main results * `Airy.coeff`: the coefficients with `y(0) = c0`, `y'(0) = c1`; `Airy.f`, `Airy.g` the two  standard solutions (`c0, c1 = 1, 0` and `0, 1`).* `Airy.coeff_airy_ode`: `d⁄dX (d⁄dX (mk (coeff c0 c1))) = X * mk (coeff c0 c1)`.* `Airy.Fps_airy`, `Airy.Gps_airy`: the same for the two standard solutions.
