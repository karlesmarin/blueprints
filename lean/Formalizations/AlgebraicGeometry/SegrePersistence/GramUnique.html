Marín, *Quadratic persistence of Segre varieties and a linear gap between biquadratic SOS rankand Zarankiewicz bounds* (2026): if the points `p i` make the map `D ↦ (D p_i)_i` injective on asubspace `𝒟` of matrices, then a positive semidefinite Gram matrix that vanishes on the `p i` isunique modulo `𝒟`; and the Gram matrix `G = ∑ j, c j • u j u jᵀ` (`c j > 0`, `u j ⬝ p i = 0`) of`F = ∑ j c j u_j²` (in the note `c = (1, …, 1, 7)`) is positive semidefinite, kills the `p i`,and has rank `e` (the number of `u j`) when the `u j` are linearly independent. Everything is over `ℝ` with an arbitrary finite index type `n` (in the note `n = Fin (m * n)`). ## Main results * `SegrePersistence.GramUnique.mulVec_eq_zero`: `G` PSD and `pᵀ G p = 0` give `G p = 0`  (this is Mathlib's `Matrix.PosSemidef.dotProduct_mulVec_zero_iff`, specialised to `ℝ`).* `SegrePersistence.GramUnique.eq_of_posSemidef`: two PSD matrices whose difference lies in `𝒟`  and whose quadratic forms vanish at every `p i` are equal.* `SegrePersistence.GramUnique.eq_of_posSemidef_of_form_eq`: the same, assuming only that the two  quadratic forms agree at the `p i` and that one of them vanishes there.* `SegrePersistence.GramUnique.gramOf_posSemidef`, `gramOf_mulVec_eq_zero`,  `dotProduct_gramOf_mulVec`, `gramOf_rank`: the Gram matrix of `∑ j c j u_j²`.* `SegrePersistence.GramUnique.gramOf_unique`: the combination used in the note. ## What is not covered That `𝒟` is the space of Gram relations of the Segre variety (`⋀²A* ⊗ ⋀²B*`), the existence of`qp(X)` rational simple tensors `p i` making the map injective (a Zariski-density argument), theidentification of a Gram matrix of the biquadratic form `F` with a matrix whose difference with`gramOf c u` lies in `𝒟`, and the rational count `ℓ_ℚ(F) = e + 3` (Witt cancellation and`7` not a sum of three rational squares, which goes through `seven_embedding` in Isabelle) arenot formalized here.-/
