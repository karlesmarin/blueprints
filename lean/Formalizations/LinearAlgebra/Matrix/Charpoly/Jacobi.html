The *resolvent series* `Matrix.resolventSeries M` is the matrix of formal power series whose`(i, j)` entry is `∑ₖ (Mᵏ) i j Xᵏ`. It is the two-sided inverse of `1 - X • M` in`Matrix n n R⟦X⟧`, and its trace is the generating function `∑ₖ trace (Mᵏ) Xᵏ`. *Jacobi's formula* expresses the derivative of the determinant of a matrix of polynomials as`derivative (det A) = trace (adjugate A * A.map derivative)`. Combining the two gives *Newton's identity for traces*: the logarithmic derivative of the reversedcharacteristic polynomial `Matrix.charpolyRev M = det (1 - X • M)` is minus the generating functionof the traces of the powers of `M`. No eigenvalues and no assumption on `R` are needed. ## Main definitions * `Matrix.resolventSeries M`: the matrix `∑ₖ Mᵏ Xᵏ` over `R⟦X⟧`. ## Main results * `Matrix.one_sub_X_smul_mul_resolventSeries`, `Matrix.resolventSeries_mul_one_sub_X_smul`,  `Matrix.resolventSeries_eq_inv`: `resolventSeries M = (1 - X • M)⁻¹`.* `Matrix.trace_resolventSeries`: `trace (resolventSeries M) = ∑ₖ trace (Mᵏ) Xᵏ`.* `Matrix.det_smul_resolventSeries`: `det (1 - X • M) • resolventSeries M = adjugate (1 - X • M)`.* `Matrix.derivative_det`: Jacobi's formula.* `Matrix.coe_derivative_charpolyRev_eq_neg_mul_mk_trace_pow`: Newton's identity  `(charpolyRev M)′ = - charpolyRev M * ∑ₖ trace (Mᵏ⁺¹) Xᵏ` in `R⟦X⟧`.
