The nullity of a matrix `A` is expressed as`Module.finrank K (LinearMap.ker A.mulVecLin)`; by `Matrix.rank_add_finrank_ker_mulVecLin` it equals`Fintype.card n - A.rank` when `A` has columns indexed by `n`. ## Main results * `Matrix.rank_add_finrank_ker_mulVecLin`: rank–nullity for matrices.* `Matrix.finrank_ker_mulVecLin_add_le`: if `U` is invertible, the nullity of `U + E` is at most  `E.rank`; `Matrix.finrank_ker_mulVecLin_sub_mul_le`: the nullity of `U - P * Q` is at most the  number of columns of `P`.* `Matrix.finrank_ker_mulVecLin_fromBlocks_zero₂₁_le`: the nullity of `fromBlocks A B 0 D` is at  most the sum of the nullities of `A` and `D`.* `Matrix.rank_add_rank_le_rank_fromBlocks_zero₂₁`: `A.rank + D.rank ≤ (fromBlocks A B 0 D).rank`. The subadditivity `Matrix.rank_add_le` and the upper bounds `Matrix.rank_fromBlocks_zero₂₁_le_left`,`Matrix.rank_fromBlocks_zero₂₁_le_right` live in `Formalizations.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.BlockRank`,which this file imports (same names); the reusable layer imports that smaller file directly. ## Related Mathlib has `LinearMap.rank_add_le` (cardinal rank of linear maps) but no `Matrix.rank_add_le`.Independently, `Matrix.rank_add_le` and `rank_add_rank_le_rank_fromBlocks_zero₂₁` over a fieldalso appear in `complexitylib` (<https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/complexitylib>,`Complexitylib/Algebraic/LinearAlgebra/MatrixRank/Internal.lean`).
