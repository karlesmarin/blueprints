If `V, W ≤ ι → K` are`Γ`-stable (possibly with different signs `εV`, `εW`, as long as `εV γ i * εW γ i = 1`) and`dim V + dim W > |ι|`, then the pointwise products `v * w` span `ι → K`. This is a statement ofCauchy–Davenport type. The proof: the orthogonal `U` of the span `S` for `∑ x_i y_i` is stable under the unsignedtranslations; if `S ≠ ⊤` then `U ≠ 0`, and since a vector space over an infinite field is not afinite union of proper subspaces (Mathlib's `Subspace.exists_eq_top_of_iUnion_eq_univ`), `U`contains a vector `u` with full support; `v ↦ u * v` embeds `V` into the orthogonal of `W`. ## Main results * `ConjeturaC.span_mul_eq_top`: the statement above. ## References * C.
