This complements the unoriented`G.incMatrix R`, whose Gram matrix is the signless Laplacian `D + A`(`incMatrix_mul_transpose_diag` ff.): the signs are what produce the Laplacian. ## Main definitions * `SimpleGraph.orientedIncMatrix`: the `α × Sym2 α` matrix with, in the column of an edge  `e = s(u, w)` with `u < w`, entry `+1` at row `w` (the larger endpoint), `-1` at row `u`,  and `0` elsewhere; columns of non-edges are zero. ## Main results * `SimpleGraph.orientedIncMatrix_mul_transpose`: `N * Nᵀ = D - A`. ## Implementation notes The orientation (each edge points from its smaller to its larger endpoint) is induced by thelinear order on the vertex type.
