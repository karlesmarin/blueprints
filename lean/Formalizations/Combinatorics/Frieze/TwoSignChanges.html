For width `w = 12` (`n = 15`), row`6 = ⌊w/2⌋` lies inside the printed range `1 ≤ k ≤ [w/2]` of Problem 1 of S. Tabachnikov(*A four-vertex theorem for frieze patterns?*, Math. Intelligencer 40 (2018)). We record two integerConway–Coxeter friezes of width `12` whose row-`6` differences have no zero and exactly two cyclic signchanges, so the answer to the question is negative for integer friezes within the printed range too.
