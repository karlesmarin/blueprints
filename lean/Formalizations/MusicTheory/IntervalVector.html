The interval class is written so that invariance under negation is `min_comm`;invariance is proved once for every embedding of `ℤ/12` preserving differences up to sign, andtransposition and inversion are special cases. ## Main results * `IVraw_map`: invariance under a difference-class-preserving embedding.* `IVraw_tpose`, `IVraw_invert`: the interval vector is invariant under transposition and inversion. ## References * C. Marín, *One transform and one duality: a machine-checked core of music theory in Lean 4*,  Zenodo (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.20953767.-/ @[expose] public section namespace MusicTheory open Finset namespace IntervalVector /-- Interval class of a pitch-class difference: `min(d, 12−d)`, value in `0..6`.
