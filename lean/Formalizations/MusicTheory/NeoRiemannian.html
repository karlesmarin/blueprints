This is the regular-action case of **Wielandt's theorem** on centralizers of transitive  permutation groups, which we did not find in Mathlib. * **Concrete (CFS duality).** The T/I action is faithful (`decide`-level) and transitive,  so it is the **left regular representation** on the 24 triads. The neo-Riemannian `P, L, R` all lie  in its centralizer (the `_comm` lemmas, repackaged as `Subgroup.closure {P,L,R} ≤  centralizer`), and the centralizer has at most 24 elements.
