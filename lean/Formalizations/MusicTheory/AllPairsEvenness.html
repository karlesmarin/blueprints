A double summation by parts(`abel_bridge`) writes `E V A - E V R` as a combination of the second differences of `V` and of`V 5 - V 6` with coefficients the double cumulative sums `DD` of `iv A - iv R`. A finitecheck over all subsets of a given size shows these coefficients are nonnegative, and positive offthe `T/I`-orbit of `R`. Hence, for every convex `V` decreasing at the top, the maximally even set`R` minimizes the energy, uniquely up to `T/I` when the inequalities are strict, for thepentatonic (`k = 5`), whole-tone (`6`), diatonic (`7`) and octatonic (`8`) collections. ## Main results * `abel_bridge`, `abel_nonneg`, `abel_pos`: the summation by parts and its sign.* `groundStateG`, `uniqueG`: the reduction to the finite check.* `diatonic_ground_state`, `diatonic_unique` and the pentatonic, whole-tone and octatonic  versions. ## Implementation notes The finite checks over the `k`-subsets of `ℤ/12` are proved by `decide` in the kernel on abitmask encoding of the subsets (`ofBits`, `exists_ofBits`, `iv_ofBits`, `census_iv`); no`native_decide` is used. ## References * J.
