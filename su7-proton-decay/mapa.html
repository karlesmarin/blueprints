\begin{itemize} \item An \textbf{obstruction} in the one-generation SU(7) model of Komori and Maru as published: three anomaly channels force $X_Q=-1/6$, where $U(1)'=T_{3L}+Y-(B-L)$ identically and the four dimension-6 $|\Delta B|=1$ operators are neutral; two further channels force a right-handed neutrino of charge $-1$, which only a $\mathbf{28}$ supplies (rows (2) and (3) of their Table~1). \item A \textbf{classification of the minimal escapes} inside their own group: the ladder $\mathrm{extra}(L)=(1-k)/2$, the identity $[SU(2)_L]^2U(1)'=\tfrac12\sum_jA_j$ (Proposition~1), fourteen anomaly-free three-generation assignments of which two fit their tensors; the $\mathbf{48}$ cannot host a generation because a real representation under diagonal $\pm1$ parities is vector-like (Proposition~2, a specialisation of Gogoladze--Mimura--Nandi). \item A \textbf{benchmark selected, not a model delivered}: $V''(0)=-\pi^2\zeta(3)D$ with $D$ rational per multiplet ($\mathbf{84}^{(+,+)}\mapsto5/4$, $\mathbf{48}^{(+,+)}\mapsto0$), so only case (2) satisfies both necessary conditions, at one-loop curvature. \item A selection rule in closed form: protection fails iff $q_\varphi=|A_j|/n$ (Proposition~3); $q_\varphi=3/2$ protects for every half-integer brane-quark charge. \item An audit of the instrument: their $\alpha_{\min}$ column is not reproduced (stated as an open question, not a defect); the verdict holds on a wedge of multiplicative repairs; two-loop safety is left open. \end{itemize}
