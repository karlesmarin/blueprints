What the paper contributes

A sorry-free Lean 4 path from the dilogarithm, which Mathlib cites but does not define, to a quantum state that never orthogonalizes; version 2 adds its exact floor, in Lean and in Isabelle.

  • The real dilogarithm with Euler’s reflection, Landen’s transformation, the duplication formula and Li₂(1/2) (Theorem 1); the golden-ratio values and the Lee–Yang ceff = 2/5 from a 3×3 linear system, without the five-term relation.
  • The circle: the Clausen function and Catalan’s constant G = Cl₂(π/2); the Fejér–Jackson inequality (Theorem 2) and, by Abel summation without asymptotics, Cl₂(θ) ≥ sin(θ)/2 on (0, π).
  • Speed limits: Margolus–Levitin and an L¹ form of Mandelstam–Tamm for a scalar spectral measure (Theorem 3).
  • The assembly (Theorem 4): populations 6/(π²n²) on levels Eₙ = n have divergent first and second energy moments, so both bounds give nothing, and yet the state never reaches an orthogonal state. Version 2 gives the one-line reason (the lowest level carries more than half of the weight) and shows that the Zieliński–Zych limits see exactly that (Proposition 1).
  • The exact floor (Theorem 5, Corollary 1): |S| ≥ 1 − 21−s for every zeta state pₙ ∝ n−s, also where no level dominates.
  • The mathematics is classical; the paper claims the formalization, the assembly and the place of the zeta state among the speed limits.

🚀 Start here: Theorem 1: Euler reflection, Landen, duplication and Li₂(1/2) for the real dilogarithm.

Main results:

  • Theorem 4: the weight-2 zeta state never reaches an orthogonal state
  • Corollary 1: every zeta state pₙ ∝ n⁻ˢ, s > 1, stays at least 1 − 2¹⁻ˢ away from orthogonality

🧭 Fields: where the paper lives and what it connects

Tools taken from other branches

  • Power series and real analysis → term-by-term differentiation, the Weierstrass M-test, mean-value constancy, the boundary limit ln x · ln(1−x) → 0 — Theorem 1
  • Fourier series and positive trigonometric sums → Mathlib’s Bernoulli–Fourier expansion for the cosine half; a division-free Dirichlet telescope and an interior-minimum induction; Abel summation without asymptotics — Theorem 2, Theorem 4
  • Quantum mechanics → speed limits stated for a scalar autocorrelation of a discrete spectral measure, with the cosine inequality cos x ≥ 1 − (2/π)(x + sin x) and its fractional-moment version (Zieliński–Zych) — Theorem 3, Proposition 1
  • Measure and integration (Hausdorff moment sequences) → the coefficients of Li_s as moments of a positive measure on [0, 1) (a Stieltjes transform), integrated against Re 1/(1 − tz) ≥ 1/(1 + t), and Abel’s theorem to reach the circle — Theorem 5

What the results serve

  • Quantum speed limits ← a state with divergent first and second energy moments that never orthogonalizes; what the moment-based limits see (only the lowest level) and the exact floor they miss — Theorem 4, Proposition 1, Corollary 1
  • Dilogarithm identities ← the golden-ratio values and the Lee–Yang c_eff = 2/5 without the five-term relation; the five-term relation is the companion paper — Theorem 1
  • Power series with positive coefficients on the unit circle ← the floor of |Li_s| at z = −1 for every s > 0 (classical: Fejér 1937); which shape conditions put the smallest modulus at z = −1, with exact counterexamples — Theorem 5, Corollary 1
