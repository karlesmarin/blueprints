To check by hand, in order

Each statement comes after what it uses. 🟡: only the part not formalized; 🔵: the argument, not its computations.

  1. windows with a rate per element: the floor of a real device (with f_A(T)² in the balance) — 🟡 the rest is to be checked by hand
  2. reciprocity: P_{B←A}[H] = P_{A←B}[H_rev], same coherence; the direction is not a design choice — 🔵 written proof; its computations are checked by a program
  3. 🔬 Check your link — the lab: the floor of YOUR link, a pulse, a verdict on your measurement, what to improve — 🔴 written proof only
  4. ramped couplings: G²T → J(T) = ∫m², minimal time 2√(A/S) or A/G + G/S — 🟡 the rest is to be checked by hand
  5. frontier for partial transfer: G²T·Ĩ ≥ Ψ(GT, ε) → 2 — 🟡 the rest is to be checked by hand
  6. asymptotic floor: 1 − P ≥ 2√(2γδ)/G (1 − o(1)) over all controls — 🔴 written proof only
  7. the constant is attained: ramp–arc–unload gives 1 − P ≤ ℓ₀[1/(1−α) + 1.5β] — 🔵 written proof; its computations are checked by a program
  8. a joint bound |g_A|² + |g_B|² ≤ G² changes the constant — 🔴 written proof only
