← Blueprint · Programs and data of «Certified Speed-Limit Diagnostics for Deployed Superconducting Quantum Processors»

Draft package, local preview. Everything needed to reproduce every number and figure of the paper: the analysis scripts, their outputs, the saved optimal CZ pulses (re-evaluated deterministically by verificar_cz.py and epsilon_cz.py), and the public IBM snapshots they read (Apache 2.0, Qiskit). Our code is under Apache-2.0 (LICENSE); origin of the data in NOTICE.
